## The Pitch (Spoken Script)

### 1. The Core Problem (0:00 – 0:30)

"Standard semantic caches rely on fixed cosine similarity thresholds. In enterprise LLM gateways, that approach breaks down completely.

When we swept fixed thresholds across 120 benchmark query pairs ([`D-01`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L52), [`P2-06`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L79)), our pre-stated safety target was a cache hazard rate—incorrect response reuse—under 10% ([`P2-02`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L75), [`C-13`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L41)). The best fixed threshold was 0.85 ([`P2-01`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L74), [`P2-04`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L77)), but it suffered 5 false positives across 24 hits—a 20.83% hazard rate ([`P2-01`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L74)). In fact, **zero** fixed thresholds satisfied our safety criterion ([`P2-03`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L76)). High similarity cannot detect temporal drift or subtle algorithmic inversions."

### 2. Architecture & Safe Reuse (0:30 – 1:00)

"To solve this, we built a 3-tier architecture with a two-stage StabilityClassifier gate.

First, volatile temporal queries are filtered out. For stable queries, TierRouter routes confident matches ($\text{sim} \ge 0.92$, $\text{conf} \ge 0.90$) directly to `AUTO_REUSE` ([`C-06`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L34), [`C-07`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L35)), while dissimilarity ($< 0.50$) or uncertainty ($< 0.80$) drops straight to `BYPASS` ([`C-08`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L36), [`C-09`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L37)).

In our demo, Query 3 asks for *'asymptotic time complexity of building an AVL tree'*. It matches our cached AVL seed at similarity 0.957 with 1.00 stability confidence. It resolves locally in about 50 ms in the demo run (within our measured 10.56–61.91 ms cache hit range)—delivering the 99.20% to 99.84% latency reduction we measured across all load test runs ([`LAT-05`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L193), [`LAT-01`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L187), [`LAT-02`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L188), [`LAT-03`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L189), [`DEMO-T03`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md))."

### 3. Catching the Near-Duplicate (1:00 – 1:30)

"The critical challenge is the ambiguous middle band.

Look at Query 6: *'What is the time complexity of Prim algorithm for minimum spanning trees using a priority queue?'* It queries against cached Dijkstra shortest paths. The phrasing is nearly identical; cosine similarity is 0.688. Under any naive vector cache, this triggers an immediate incorrect hit.

In our pipeline, 0.688 falls between 0.50 and 0.92, routing into `AMBIGUOUS`. An asynchronous LLM judge ([`C-14`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L42)) inspects full semantic intent. The judge detects that Prim's algorithm for spanning trees differs fundamentally from Dijkstra's shortest paths, issues a `BYPASS` decision, and safely prevents cache corruption."

### 4. Honest Blind Validation & Confidence Intervals (1:30 – 2:00)

"We validated this system on a strictly sealed blind evaluation dataset of 175 entries across 7 domains ([`D-09`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L60), [`P6-01`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L154), sealed commit `fc35744` [`P6-02`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L155)).

The pipeline achieved 8 cache hits (1 `AUTO_REUSE`, 7 judge-approved; [`P6-03`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L158)) with **0 false positives**, for an empirical hazard rate of 0.00% ([`P6-04`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L159)). Across all runs, 86.39% to 89.71% of queries resolved locally without calling remote LLMs ([`LRR-04`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L205)).

Here is our honest statistical takeaway: with $n=8$ blind hits, the exact 95% Clopper-Pearson CI is [0.00%, 36.94%] ([`P6-05`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L160)). When pooled with our 338-query scaled run ([`D-08`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L59), [`P5B-02`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L135)), we have $n=22$ hits ([`POOL-02`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L175)) and $k=0$ errors ([`POOL-01`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L174)), bringing the CI upper bound down to 15.44% ([`POOL-03`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L176)). But 15.44% is still above our 10% ceiling ([`POOL-06`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L178)). Mathematically, proving $IRR \le 10\%$ at 95% confidence requires at least 36 defect-free hits ([`P4-04`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L105), [`KL-02`](file:///c:/Users/parid/Downloads/Agentic%20AI/adaptive-agentic-semantic-cache/docs/FACTS.md#L214)). We built the safety architecture and proved zero empirical hazards, but we do not claim statistical proof where the sample size does not warrant it."
